Our Story

Helping Eastern Iowa find home for over 30 years.

Kelley Property Management has been serving housing needs in the Cedar Rapids, Marion and Hiawatha areas for over 30 years. With over 1,000 rental units to choose from, we can accommodate all of your housing needs.

Our large selection ranges from one to three bedroom apartments, town homes, duplexes, houses and condos to commercial spaces. Not only are we one of the largest property management firms in the area with the largest selection — we also offer the lowest rent.

All properties are locally owned and managed by professional office and maintenance staff. Visit our main office for a private showing of available units — no appointment necessary.
